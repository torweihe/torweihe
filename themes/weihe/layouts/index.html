Hi! – and welcome to my website. My name is Tór Marni and I am a PhD candidate in International Politics at the University of the Faroe Islands, researching foreign, security, and defence policy in the North Atlantic and Arctic region.

My research focuses on the Faroe Islands and Greenland as case studies for what I call parasecurity — a concept I am developing to explain how non-sovereign autonomous territories conduct security diplomacy within larger constitutional arrangements.

I also teach an MA-level course on small states and international relations, and I am working to establish the Faroese Centre for International Affairs as an independent research institution.

My work engages with hedging theory, shelter theory, sovereignty studies, Arctic geopolitics, and the constitutional arrangements of the Danish Realm.
